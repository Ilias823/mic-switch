Mic Switch for DaVinci Resolve

Turns each interview mic off while the other person talks, so you only hear the speaker's own lav and no bleed.

It works on a copy of your timeline and only disables clip pieces (press D to bring one back), so your edit is never changed.

You need:

  • A Mac with macOS 13 or later
  • DaVinci Resolve 21.1.2 or later
  • Each person on their own audio track, recorded as .wav

Nothing else is needed: no Python packages, no AI.
