Mic Switch is installed

Mic Switch is free and open source. If it saves you time, you can support its development:

☕ Support on Ko-fi
  1. Restart DaVinci Resolve if it is open.
  2. Open your interview timeline.
  3. Choose Workspace > Scripts > Mic Switch.
  4. Tick the mic tracks and press Create Timeline.

If it says macOS blocked the audio file: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders, allow DaVinci Resolve to use the drive with your audio.

To remove it, run in Terminal:
sudo "/Library/Application Support/MicSwitch/uninstall.sh"
